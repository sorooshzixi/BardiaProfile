About Me

Ever since I can remember, I have loved to express myself through various mediums, be it a drawing I had done during PE, or entire scene crafted over 6 months using CG. At the heart of my professional journey is a blend of passion and discipline that drives every pixel, line of code, and design. I have worn many hats over the years, from a Technical Designer to an Indie Game Developer. My adventure has taught me the value of authenticity, adaptability, and humor in my professional life. While I'm proud of the skills I've honed, I understand the importance of staying grounded and continually evolving. Whether it's delving into the depths of a new software or brainstorming with my coworkers, my commitment is unwavering: to deliver with integrity, diligence, and a touch of playful innovation.
